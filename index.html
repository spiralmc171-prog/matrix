Regulations

SERVER RULES

GENERAL RULES

  • Respect all players and staff at all times.
  • No hacking, exploiting or unauthorized mods.
  • Do not spam chat or use offensive language.
  • Follow staff instructions at all times.
  • No advertising other servers.

ROLEPLAY RULES

  • Stay in character (IC) during roleplay at all times.
  • No powergaming or metagaming allowed.
  • Fear RP must be maintained in life-threatening situations.
  • Do not break character without /ooc notice.
  • Random Deathmatch (RDM) is strictly prohibited.

GANG RULES

  • Gang wars must be initiated via proper RP.
  • No drive-by without prior RP interaction.
  • Respect gang territory and boundaries.
  • No excessive killing in non-war zones.
  • Gang recruitment must follow guidelines.

FACTION RULES

  • Members must follow their faction SOP.
  • Abuse of faction powers results in punishment.
  • Corrupt RP must be approved by senior staff.
  • Faction vehicles are for official use only.
  • Log all major faction operations properly.

VOICE RULES

  • Use voice only in designated IC situations.
  • No mic spamming or playing music through voice.
  • Maintain appropriate volume levels.
  • No harassment via voice channels.

VEHICLE RULES

  • No vehicle deathmatch (VDM) under any circumstances.
  • Park vehicles properly in designated areas.
  • No stealing faction vehicles without RP reason.
  • Racing must be done in designated zones.

PUNISHMENT SYSTEM

  • 1st offense: Verbal warning via admin PM.
  • 2nd offense: Kick from server.
  • 3rd offense: Temporary ban (1–7 days).
  • Severe violations: Permanent ban without warning.
  • Appeals submitted via Discord ticket.
